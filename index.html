Welcome to Run Advent!

25 days. 25 presents. Your own Christmas running adventure.

  1. Every day, December 1–25, a new present unlocks at midnight.
  2. Open your present to reveal that day’s surprise running distance.
  3. Complete your run and mark it done to earn progress and achievements.
  4. Go at your pace: catch up on previous days, share progress with friends and celebrate at the finish.
🎯 Three difficulty levelsBeginner 75 km · Intermediate 125 km · Advanced 325 km25 surprises in every challenge. Optional distance swaps, or Strict Mode for no swaps.
